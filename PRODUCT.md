# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted about equally (confirmed by the owner):

- Hiring engineers and recruiters screening for internships and full-time roles in embedded systems, RF and robotics. They skim, often in under a minute.
- Professors and research labs judging fit for a master's programme, research internship or lab position.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Saikirtan Srinivasan, an Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at SSN College of Engineering, Chennai (graduating 2027). It exists so a visitor can judge his work and get in touch. Success is a visitor leaving with one clear impression and a reason to make contact.

## Positioning

The one thing a visitor should remember (confirmed): he works across the whole stack, from PCB and RF front end, through firmware, up to a robot's navigation software. The evidence is one person's projects at each layer, not a claim.

## Operating Context

Reached from a resume link, LinkedIn or GitHub. Read on a laptop by engineers and faculty, and on a phone by recruiters. Single page, static HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on Vercel and auto-deployed from the GitHub repository `Saikirtan-Srinivasan/Saikirtan-Srinivasan.github.io`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections today: intro, about, experience, projects, skills, education, achievements and leadership, contact. Sections and wording may be rewritten, merged or reordered; facts must stay true to the resume.
- Contact is email, LinkedIn and GitHub only. The phone number stays off the site (owner's choice).
- No build step, no framework.
- Undecided: whether the `minios-8bit-cpu` repository should appear as a project (it is not on the resume).

## Brand Commitments

- The owner's profile photo stays on the page (`assets/photo.webp`).
- A polished result is wrong if it is flashy or gimmicky, looks like a generic developer-portfolio template, reads like a resume pasted onto a page, or is too casual for a recruiter to take seriously.

## Product Principles

1. Show range through the work at each layer, not through adjectives.
2. A skimming recruiter and a careful professor must both be served: the top answers "who and what" in seconds, the depth is there for whoever keeps reading.
3. Only resume-true facts. Missing evidence is left out, not invented.
4. Serious without being stiff; nothing that reads as a gimmick.
