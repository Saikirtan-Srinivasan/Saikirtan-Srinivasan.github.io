# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: hiring engineers/recruiters and professors, weighted equally; judge the work, then make contact. Proof: resume-true projects and internships only; no project imagery exists. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, photo stays, phone number stays off, nothing flashy, generic, plain or casual.

Chosen direction: Layer Stack, picked by the owner from a safer hand (kind: grounded). Memorable moment: the stack diagram that is both the first-viewport picture of the work and the page's navigation, with projects split across the layers they touch.

Unresolved: whether `minios-8bit-cpu` joins as a project.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one engineer's stack. Five full-width layers, Autonomy at the top down to RF at the bottom, each holding the work done at that layer; a project that crosses layers is split and threaded across them. Refuses the name-tagline-photo hero over a grid of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: A depth ramp in one blue-green hue family: palest at Autonomy, darkening layer by layer to deep ink at RF, on a cool paper-white ground, with one vermilion signal colour for actions, threads and status. Archivo only, using its width axis: extra-condensed heavy caps for layer names, normal width for reading text, condensed semibold caps for codes and tool lists. Hairline rules, square corners, no cards, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole stack at once, understands that one person works at every layer, descends through the evidence, and reaches contact at the bottom.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: name in large condensed caps, one sentence stating the range, photo, three contact actions. Right column, about 55% of the width and nearly full height: the five-layer stack diagram in the ramp colours, each bar carrying its layer name and the codes and names of the work in it, every bar a link. Primary action: Email, vermilion, at the foot of the left column beside the photo, kept together with the GitHub and LinkedIn links so the three ways to reach him read as one group.

FORM: Layer Stack, my second-ranked grounded candidate. seed:5f8519756a1c1c07. Signature interaction: the stack diagram persists as a fixed side rail (a five-segment bar on phones) that lights the current layer and jumps between layers; cross-layer threads link a project's parts. Motion grammar: the stack bars settle into place bottom-up once on load; nothing else animates beyond state changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
