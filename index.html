01About

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at SSN College of Engineering, Chennai, graduating in 2027. I like working where hardware meets software: firmware on microcontrollers, RF front ends, PCB layout and robots that find their own way around.

Over two internships I've built LiDAR-based autonomous navigation on Raspberry Pi and a ROS 2 hospital delivery robot that takes its destination by voice. Outside of those, my team's thermal-imaging people counter placed in the national Top 10 at the Silicon Labs Edge Intelligence Challenge 2026, and I'm currently designing a 2.4 GHz zero-IF software defined radio receiver.

I'm looking for opportunities in embedded systems, RF and robotics where I can keep working close to the hardware.
